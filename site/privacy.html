English

This app calculates Diablo IV world boss, Helltide and Legion event times and sends reminders.

1. Personal data collected by the app itself: none. The app does not directly collect your name, email, location or contacts. There is no login.

2. On-device settings: Alarm on/off, lead time, ringtone and quiet hours are stored only on your device and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

3. Internet use: The app reads a public schedule page (helltides.com) for event times and boss names, and a public file on the developer's site (d4alarm.stackupinfra.com) for season info. No user information is sent.

4. Feedback (only when you send it): Only when you tap Send in the Feedback tab, your message, the star rating you chose, the app version, Android version, phone maker and model, and a random code made by the app (e.g. K7Q2M) are delivered to the developer through Google Forms. No name, email or contact details are sent, so the sender can't be identified. It is used only to improve the app and is never sold or shared. If the developer replies, only the random code and the reply are posted in a public sheet, which the app reads to show the reply to the sender. To have your feedback deleted, email the developer with its random code.

5. Clan recruiting (only when a clan leader registers): Only when a clan leader taps Register, the clan name, one-line intro, activity time, open chat/Discord links, BattleTag, a contact the leader enters, and a random code made by the app are sent to the developer's Google Sheet. Listings are free, go live immediately and are removed automatically after 15 days; everything except the contact is shown to app users. A report sends only the reason, the clan number and a random code made by the app (used to count one phone once). To delete a registration, email the developer.

6. Ads and analytics: The app has no ads (since 3.9) and uses no ad SDK or analytics tools. Clan listings are marked as promotions.

6-1. Anonymous statistics: When a clan promotion is shown or its open-chat/Discord button is tapped, only the clan number and counts (no value that identifies a device or user) are sent to the developer's spreadsheet. A phone that registered a clan checks its application status with the server periodically while it is waiting.

7. Sharing with third parties: None. Feedback and clan applications reach the developer through Google Forms and Sheets (see 4 and 5).

8. Children: The app does not collect information from children.

9. Contact: Please use the developer email shown on the Google Play listing.

This is a fan-made app and is not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.
